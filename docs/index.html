Your voice comes first

You define your creative vision in the Author Manifest—Distil's foundational document. This is where you articulate genre, tone, themes, narrative priorities, and stylistic preferences.

The AI reads and respects these instructions in every interaction. When you ask for scene development, character dialogue, or story continuity checks, the AI works within the boundaries you've set—never straying from your intent.

Your creative direction isn't a suggestion to the AI—it's a contract. The Author Manifest ensures your voice remains the guiding force, with the AI operating only as an assistant to your vision.

Write with confidence

Long-form fiction demands that characters, places, and world details remain consistent across hundreds of pages. Distil tracks entities and context throughout your story, enabling the AI to reference continuity when helping you write.

When you ask for a scene involving a specific character or location, the AI draws from established details—personality traits, past events, setting descriptions—to maintain internal consistency.

You can also create structured guides, outlines, or world-building documents that further inform the AI's responses. The system is designed to keep your narrative coherent, so you can focus on storytelling rather than tracking details manually.
